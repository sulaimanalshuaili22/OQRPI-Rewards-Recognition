# OQ RPI · Talent Management — narration script & timing sheet

**Delivery:** executive documentary — confident, warm, measured; inspirational, never promotional.
**Pace:** ~1.3 s phrases with ~0.5 s pauses at clause breaks; let the picture breathe between lines.

Timecodes are where each line starts in the finished film (mm:ss.s). The picture is cut to these.

**Pronunciation (no exceptions):**
- **OQ** — two separate letters: "O" … "Q" (never "ock").
- **RPI** — spelled out: "R" "P" "I".
- **ROBBAN** — "Ro-bban" (ROH-bahn), not "Ra-bban".
- **MASAR** — "Ma-SAAR".
- **9-Box** — "nine-box".
