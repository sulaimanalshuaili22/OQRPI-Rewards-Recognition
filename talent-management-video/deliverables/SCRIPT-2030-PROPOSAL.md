# Narration script, proposed: RPI 2030 Transformation

Proposal only: no change has been made to the film. It is based on the RPI 2030 Transformation house (Level 3, People & Culture: *"Performance driven, customer centric culture · Future-ready organization · Strong collaboration and organizational agility"*; tagline *"Strengthening the core, while embracing the future"*).

Key: **NEW** = added line · **CHANGED** = edited approved line · unmarked = approved line, unchanged.

## 01 · The future begins with people (0:00)
1. Every successful organisation shares one common strength… its people.
2. At OQ RPI, behind every operation, every innovation, and every achievement, lies talent.

*On screen: the title card's proposition line becomes "RPI 2030 Transformation · People & Culture · Future-ready organization".*

## 02 · Why Talent Management: the 2030 anchor
3. **NEW:** OQ RPI's 2030 Transformation has a clear direction: strengthening the core, while embracing the future.
4. **NEW:** At its heart, the People and Culture pillar commits us to one outcome Talent Management exists to deliver: a future-ready organization.
5. In a fast-changing energy sector, OQ RPI must have the right talent, in the right roles, at the right time.
6. That means knowing which roles are critical, who is ready to step into them, and where capability must be built.
7. OQ RPI Talent Management exists to build sustainable workforce capability, strengthen our leadership pipeline, and secure the future of our business.

*On screen for 3–4: the 2030 Transformation house builds. The other priorities dim, People & Culture lifts forward, and "Future-ready organization" lights in orange. That word becomes the destination of the "One capability framework" spine in line 7 (its last node, "Future Readiness", is renamed "Future-ready organization").*

## 03 · The ecosystem
8. At OQ RPI, Talent Management is not a collection of separate programmes. It is one integrated ecosystem.
9. **CHANGED:** Brought together in our Talent Command Center, each programme generates insights, actions and data that strengthen the next, **all aimed at one goal: a future-ready organization.**

*On screen: the cycle's centre reads "One integrated ecosystem · serving a future-ready organization".*

## 04 · Performance Management
10. Performance Management enables us to assess, measure, and recognise employee contribution while supporting strategic talent decisions.
11. **CHANGED:** It is the foundation for every talent decision we make, **and for the performance-driven culture our 2030 Transformation calls for.**

## 05–13 · Programmes, Command Center, connections: unchanged
12. Through our annual Talent Review, the 9-Box Matrix combines performance and potential to identify our future leaders and accelerate their development.
13. Not every position carries the same level of risk.
14. Our Critical Roles framework identifies the positions whose vacancy would impact safety, operations, leadership continuity, or business performance.
15. Succession Planning identifies successors for critical positions, then develops them until they are ready, to support business continuity.
16. Leadership development programmes such as MASAR and ROBBAN build future leaders and strengthen organisational capability.
17. Nationalization develops and advances Omani talent into critical roles and leadership positions, with a named successor for every role we nationalize.
18. Through Secondment Management, OQ RPI employees gain broader exposure across OQ and national institutions, accelerating their learning and bringing new capability home.
19. Rewards and Recognition, from Testahal to Above and Beyond, reinforces the behaviours we value and celebrates the people who make our success possible.
20. The OQ RPI Talent Command Center is the central platform that brings it all together: Performance Management, Succession Planning, Critical Roles, Leadership Development, Nationalization, Secondment, and Rewards and Recognition, giving every leader real-time visibility.
21. And our Talent Assistant answers any question on talent data, in English or Arabic.
22. Together, these initiatives form the OQ RPI Talent Management ecosystem, transforming data into decisions, potential into capability, and employees into future leaders.
23. For every employee, it means clear expectations, real development, and a visible path to grow with OQ RPI.

## 14 · The future of OQ RPI
24. The future is not built by systems. It is built by people.
25. **CHANGED:** By investing in our people today, OQ RPI is protecting critical capability, building its next generation of leaders, and advancing Omani talent, **the future-ready organization our 2030 Transformation calls for.**
26. OQ RPI Talent Management. Building tomorrow's talent, together.

*On screen: after Continuity, Leadership and National Talent rise over the refinery, a fourth line settles beneath them: "Future-ready organization · RPI 2030". The end card adds the small line "Serving the RPI 2030 Transformation".*

---

**Effect:** 2 new lines and 3 edited lines. "Future-ready organization" is spoken 3 times: at the start, at the ecosystem and at the close. Narration grows by about 22–26 s, to roughly 5:02 (scenes re-time automatically to the new read, with the same approved voice). Every new wording quotes the 2030 house directly; nothing is added beyond it.
